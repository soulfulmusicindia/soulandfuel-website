## Full post, ready to paste

Most hotel bathrooms give me about two feet to stand in.

That is the whole studio.

Tripod in the doorway, one foot out in the corridor, camera pushed back against the frame. That is where the picture gets made.

People blame the lens. Go wide enough and the room bends. Taps stretch. The wall nearest the camera swells up and the far one runs away. A guest sees that picture, books the room, walks in and feels lied to.

Where you stand does more than the lens does.

I keep the camera square to the far wall and level, so the vertical lines stay vertical. One step forward and the shower is gone. One step back and the door frame eats the corner. There is usually one spot in the room that works. Finding it takes ten minutes.

Then the mirror. Every bathroom has one and it is aimed at me. So I keep moving until the mirror is holding tile or a towel rail and not a tripod. Some days that means losing the composition I wanted.

This picture works hard for a hotel. When someone is comparing two rooms online, the bathroom is often the frame that decides it. If it is not clear, they assume the room is small and not clean.

So give it time on the shoot day. Four square metres, and it can take forty minutes.

How long does your team put aside for bathrooms? Curious what that number looks like at your property.

## Things to check before posting

1. The numbers are mine, not yours. Two feet of standing room, ten minutes to find the spot, forty minutes per bathroom. They read as true for a compact hotel bathroom but please correct them to whatever your actual figures are before this goes out.
2. The claim that the bathroom frame is often what decides a booking is written as your experience, not as a statistic. Change it if you would rather not say it.
3. Both chosen images have an artificial light visibly on. A pendant in the green bathroom, and the mirror LED in the hotel one. The post says nothing about lighting so this does not clash with anything written, but if someone asks in the comments how you light bathrooms, be ready for it.
4. Confirm you are happy posting the hotel bathroom publicly. It is the only unwatermarked bathroom in the repo and I do not know what was agreed with that client.
