Two instincts, one camera.

The studio

I shoot spaces the way I shoot rivers — paying attention to where the light actually wants to go.

Soul & Fuel is a one-person photography and film studio working across three lanes: interior & architecture photography, travel films and corporate films. They look like different jobs. In practice they're the same instinct, pointed at different subjects — read the space, find the honest angle, then get out of the way.

The name is the whole philosophy. Soul is what a place or a journey actually feels like once you've stopped performing for the camera. FUEL is the discipline that gets you the shot anyway — the scouting, the timing, the gear, the deadline. Neither one works without the other.

Based in Bengaluru. Available across India and abroad for the right project.

Questions clients ask

What does Soul & Fuel Media shoot?

Mostly interior and architecture photography: homes, apartments, offices, showrooms, cafes, restaurants, hotels and resorts. We also make travel films and corporate films.

Do you photograph hotels and resorts?

Yes. Recent hospitality shoots include Aalterra Resort and The Old Acre in Wayanad, Magnolia Resorts in Coorg, Hotel GSR in Tumkur and Yolotel in Bengaluru, covering rooms, common areas, exteriors and grounds.

Who do you usually work with?

Architects, interior designers, real estate developers and hotel brands. Designers we shoot for include Style My Kona, Eden Elements, Syzygy Designz, Inspacio Design Studio, Tesor Designs, Maysons, Kham Design and Manmeet Bevli Designs.

Where are you based, and do you travel for shoots?

We're based in Bengaluru and work across India, including Mumbai. Recent projects took us to Coorg, Chikmagalur, Wayanad, Kochi and Hyderabad.

Which magazines have published your work?

ELLE Decor India, Architect & Interiors India, Good Homes India, India Today Homes, The New Indian Express and Home Publication.

Do you shoot video as well as photographs?

Yes. We shoot interior walkthrough videos, travel films and corporate and brand films, with aerial footage where it helps tell the story.

How do I book a shoot?

Email mahesh@soulandfuel.com, call or WhatsApp +91 79049 99975, or use the contact form. Tell us about the space, where it is and roughly when you need it shot, and we'll reply within a couple of days. Studio hours are Monday to Saturday, 9:30 am to 7 pm.
